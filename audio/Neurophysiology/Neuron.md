## Neuron

Let's start with the neuron, which is the structural and functional unit of the nervous system.
It is an electrically excitable cell, specialised for receiving, integrating and transmitting information.
It does this through two kinds of signal, namely electrical signals, in the form of action potentials, and chemical signals at synapses.
Its main components are the cell body, the dendrites and the axon, and we'll take each one in turn.

## Cell Body (Soma)

To begin with, the cell body, also called the soma, is the metabolic and synthetic centre of the neuron.
It contains the organelles, namely the ribosomes, the mitochondria and the endoplasmic reticulum, which are responsible for protein synthesis and energy production.
The soma also integrates incoming signals, and it maintains the neuron's survival.
Located within the soma is one important subcomponent, the nucleus.
The nucleus contains the genetic material, that is the DNA, and it controls all cellular activity.
This includes gene expression for the synthesis of neurotransmitters.

## Dendrites

Now, let's turn to the dendrites, which are branched processes, shaped a little like trees, that extend out from the soma.
They are the main receptive structures of the neuron.
In other words, they receive incoming signals from other neurons at synapses, and they conduct those signals towards the cell body.
Their extensive branching vastly increases the surface area available for synaptic input.

## Axon

That brings us to the axon, a single, elongated projection of the neuron.
The axon conducts signals away from the soma, onwards to other neurons, muscles or glands.
It carries action potentials over distances ranging from millimetres to over a metre.
The axon has its own key subcomponent as well, known as the axon hillock.
The axon hillock is the cone shaped junction between the soma and the axon.
It acts as the neuron's spike initiation zone.
In practice, it sums, or integrates, all of the incoming excitatory and inhibitory postsynaptic potentials.
If the threshold is reached here, an action potential is generated.

## In short

So, to bring it all together, the dendrites receive, the soma integrates, the axon hillock triggers, and the axon transmits.
Keep that sequence in mind, as it captures how the neuron receives, integrates and transmits information.
