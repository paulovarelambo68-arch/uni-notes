
>==REM sleep and slow wave sleep (SWS) are the two contrasting states of sleep==, defined by their [[EEG and the Stages of Sleep | EEG signatures ]]and by opposite physiological profiles. SWS is a state of bodily rest with a regular autonomic rhythm, while REM combines muscle paralysis with autonomic activation.

### Slow Wave Sleep

In SWS there is ==substantial muscle tone==, although it is **reduced from wakefulness**, and ==body movements remain frequent==. ==Heart rate, blood pressure and respiration are maintained at a regular rate==, so autonomic activity stays steady throughout this stage. Of the three non REM stages, only ==N3==, the deepest, is considered SWS.

### REM Sleep

In REM sleep there is a ==pronounced loss of muscle tone==, leaving the body effectively **paralysed**. ==Heart rate, blood pressure and respiration show sharp fluctuations== instead of a regular rhythm, ==rapid eye movements== give the stage its name, and ==brain temperature is raised compared with SWS==. It has been suggested that **dreams** might reflect exactly those sharp autonomic fluctuations.
