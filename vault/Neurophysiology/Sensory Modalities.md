
> A ==sensory modality== is each unique type of sensation: every distinct quality of experience, such as touch, pain or vision, that the nervous system detects through its own class of receptor.

### Somatic modalities

The somatic modalities gather information from the skin and deep tissues. ==Touch, pressure and vibration== are mechanical stimuli detected by ==mechanoreceptors==, which are distributed through the ==epidermis, the dermis and the deep tissue==, so mechanical energy is sampled at different depths. Alongside these sit ==proprioception==, the sense of the position and movement of the body, ==temperature==, detected by ==thermoreceptors==, and ==pain==, detected by ==nociceptors==.

### Special senses and visceral modalities

The same receptor logic extends to the special senses and the viscera. Mechanical stimuli are also detected by ==hair cells==, which mediate ==hearing and balance==, and by ==baroreceptors==, which monitor ==blood pressure==. ==Electromagnetic radiation== is detected by ==rods and cones==, while ==chemoreceptors== cover ==taste and smell== together with internal chemical variables such as ==[[pO₂, pCO₂]] and glucose==.
