
A a local change in [[Membrane Potential|membrane potential]] whose amplitude is proportional to the strength of the stimulus: the stronger the stimulus, the larger the depolarisation. It is graded because its size varies continuously rather than being all or none, and it is decremental, fading as it spreads passively from where it was generated, so it cannot carry information over distance. Its function is to act as a trigger: if the depolarisation reaches threshold, it initiates an [[The Action Potential|action potential]], and the stimulus is then encoded in firing frequency.
