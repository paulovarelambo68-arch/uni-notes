
Neurotransmitters (or their synthesising enzymes) are proteins or protein-derived molecules, and proteins are built according to the genetic code. The nucleus controls this via the classic **DNA → RNA → protein** pathway:

1. A stimulus (e.g., repeated neural activity) activates **transcription factors** in the nucleus.
2. These bind to DNA and switch **on the transcription** of a specific gene into messenger RNA (mRNA).
3. The mRNA travels to ribosomes in the soma, where it is **translated** into a protein.
