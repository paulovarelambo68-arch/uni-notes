
The soma contains nearly all the neuron's protein-making machinery, like ribosomes, rough endoplasmic reticulum (visible under the microscope as _Nissl bodies_), the Golgi apparatus, and lots of mitochondria. Neurons are among the most metabolically demanding cells in the body: they constantly need to manufacture **enzymes, ion channels, membrane receptors, neurotransmitter vesicles, and cytoskeletal proteins**. Almost all of this is produced in the soma and then shipped down the axon via **axonal transport** (like a factory with a delivery railway). If the axon is cut, the distal segment degenerates because it can't sustain itself without the soma.
