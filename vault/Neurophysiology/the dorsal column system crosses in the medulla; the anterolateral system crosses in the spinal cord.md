
**Spinal cord:** the long bundle of nerve tissue running down your spine (neck to lower back), connecting brain to body for movement and sensation.

**Medulla oblongata (medulla):** the lowest part of the brainstem, just above where the spinal cord begins. It controls automatic functions like breathing, heart rate, and swallowing.

How to tell them apart:

1. Location: medulla sits inside the skull, above the foramen magnum; spinal cord sits below it, inside the spine.
2. Function: medulla = automatic life functions; spinal cord = signal relay highway between brain and body.
3. Rule of thumb: medulla = brain tissue, spinal cord = nerve cable below the brain.
